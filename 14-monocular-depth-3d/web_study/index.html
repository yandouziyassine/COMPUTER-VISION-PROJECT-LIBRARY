Which point is closer?

Part of a study comparing human and machine depth perception from a single photo. For each photo two points are marked, A and B. Choose the one on the surface that is closer to the camera.

  • If a point is on a mirror, window or picture, judge the surface itself (the glass or the print), not what you see in it.
  • Answer quickly, on your first impression (about 2–3 seconds). There is always a correct answer.
  • On about half of the photos the picture is shown for only a fraction of a second and then covered by noise; the two points stay marked. Answer from what you saw.
  • Some photos are shown upside down, in gray or slightly blurred. That is intended.
  • 300 photos, about 15–20 minutes. Keys: A / B (or the arrow keys).

Anonymous: no name, e-mail or other personal data is asked or recorded. Only your choices, response times and a random session code are kept, and only if you send the file at the end. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
